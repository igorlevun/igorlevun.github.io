Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Eureka Dashboard ("the App") is a personal content-management tool operated by its owner. This policy explains what data the App handles when it connects to TikTok.

1. Data we access

When you connect a TikTok account, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (such as display name and avatar) through TikTok's official Login Kit. The App uses TikTok's Content Posting API to upload video content that you choose to upload.

2. How data is used

Access tokens are used solely to authenticate API requests to TikTok on your behalf (for example, uploading a video you selected). Profile information is used only to display which account is connected.

3. Storage

Tokens and settings are stored locally on the owner's own machine. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

4. Data retention and deletion

You can disconnect the App at any time from your TikTok settings ("Security & permissions" → "Apps and services"), which revokes its access. Locally stored tokens can be deleted at any time by the owner.

5. Third-party services

The App interacts with TikTok through its official APIs. TikTok's handling of your data is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.
